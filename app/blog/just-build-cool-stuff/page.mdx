### Why Building Stuff Matters More Than Anything?

I was thinking lately—why do all the people who went on to achieve great things in life have one thing in common?

**And that is:**

The passion to build stuff from scratch.

When you build anything from scratch, you stay grounded in reality. You understand the pros and cons of every decision because you’re in the trenches, solving real problems, not just theorizing.

Take Steve Jobs, for example. &#x20;

He didn’t just have ideas—*he built them.* From the first Apple computer in his garage to the iPhone that changed the world, Jobs was obsessed with creating. He cared about every detail, from the design to how it felt in your hand. That’s what made Apple more than just another tech company.

**Here’s the thing**

When you build, you learn. You fail. You adapt. And eventually, you create something that can change the world—or at least your world.

So, if you want to do something meaningful, stop overthinking. &#x20;

***Start building.***
